I am a postdoctoral fellow at the [Zhu Lab](https://zhulab.seas.umich.edu/) (University of Michigan) and the [Blesh Lab](https://bleshlab.com/) (Michigan State University). My research focuses on nutrient cycling, plant communities, and microbial interactions under global change and different agricultural practices. <br>

I did my PhD at University of Texas at Austin, co-advised by Caroline Farrior and Amy Wolf, where I studied how soil microbes influence plant diversity and productivity. <br>

Here, you can read about my ecology projects 🌲, instructional design materials 📝, and artworks 🎨.
